Disrupting colonoscopy

Our Magnetic Flexible Endoscope replaces the traditional push-style scope with a soft, capsule-shaped tip guided through the colon by an external magnet on a robotic arm. The aim: painless, safer, easier and more affordable investigation of the large intestine — and earlier detection of cancer.

Intelligent magnetic manipulation

Computer vision and control let the system autonomously navigate inside the body, with the clinician always able to take over — pushing toward affordable, intelligent endoscopy for everyone.
