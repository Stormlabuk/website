The **STORM Lab** (Science and Technology Of Robotics in Medicine) develops
intelligent robotic technology for medicine. Our research spans several fields of
surgical robotics, with particular focus on **medical capsule robots, robotic
endoscopy, soft surgical robots, affordable medical devices and autonomy in
robotic surgery**.

We strive to enable earlier diagnosis, wider screening and more effective
treatment for life-threatening diseases such as cancer. By creating affordable
and intelligent robotic solutions, we aim to improve the quality of life for
people undergoing flexible endoscopy and laparoscopic surgery — including in
settings with limited access to healthcare infrastructure.

## Two labs, one mission

STORM Lab operates on both sides of the Atlantic:

- **STORM Lab UK**, based at the University of Leeds, directed by Professor Pietro Valdastri.
- **STORM Lab USA**, directed by Keith Obstein.

The two groups collaborate closely on the science and translation of robotics in
medicine, combining engineering and clinical expertise.

## Our approach

We hold deep expertise in the **magnetic manipulation and localisation** of
medical capsules and soft robots, which we use to reach deep inside the human
body to diagnose and treat life-threatening and inoperable diseases. We pair this
with **computer vision, control and autonomy** so that robotic systems can
navigate safely — always under clinical oversight.

> Affordable, intelligent robotics that widen access to diagnosis and treatment.
