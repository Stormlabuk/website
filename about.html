About STORM Lab UK

Our expertise

We magnetically manipulate and localize medical capsules and soft robots to reach deep inside the human body and treat life-threatening, inoperable disease.

Professor & Chair in Robotics and Autonomous Systems · IEEE Fellow · Ph.D. in Medical Robotics, Scuola Superiore Sant'Anna
