About us

Science and Technology Of Robotics in Medicine

Intelligent, affordable robotics for earlier diagnosis and more effective, less invasive treatment.

The STORM Lab develops intelligent robotic technology for medicine. Our research spans several fields of surgical robotics, with particular focus on medical capsule robots, robotic endoscopy, soft surgical robots, affordable medical devices and autonomy in robotic surgery.

We strive to enable earlier diagnosis, wider screening and more effective treatment for life-threatening diseases such as cancer. By creating affordable and intelligent robotic solutions, we aim to improve the quality of life for people undergoing flexible endoscopy and laparoscopic surgery — including in settings with limited access to healthcare infrastructure.

Two labs, one mission

STORM Lab operates on both sides of the Atlantic: STORM Lab UK, based at the University of Leeds and directed by Professor Pietro Valdastri, and STORM Lab USA, directed by Keith Obstein. The two groups collaborate closely on the science and translation of robotics in medicine.

Our approach

We hold deep expertise in the magnetic manipulation and localisation of medical capsules and soft robots, which we use to reach deep inside the human body to diagnose and treat life-threatening and inoperable diseases. We pair this with computer vision, control and autonomy so that robotic systems can navigate safely — always under clinical oversight.

“We strive to improve the quality of life for people undergoing flexible endoscopy and laparoscopic surgery by creating affordable and intelligent robotic solutions.”
