Medical capsule robots are small devices that can be swallowed or introduced into
the body to reach places that are difficult or dangerous for conventional
instruments. STORM Lab is a world leader in the **magnetic actuation and
localisation** of these capsules.

## Magnetic manipulation and localisation

By combining external magnetic fields with on-board sensing, we can **move,
orient and track** a capsule deep inside the gastrointestinal tract without rigid
mechanical linkages. This lets us reach anatomy that rigid endoscopes cannot, and
do so more gently.

## From diagnosis to therapy

Our capsules are designed not only to **see** (imaging and diagnostics) but
increasingly to **act** — carrying tools and sensors that enable biopsy,
targeted treatment and other interventions, all under precise magnetic control.

This work underpins several of our other programmes, including the
[Magnetic Flexible Endoscope](/research/mfe/) and
[advanced sensing](/research/sensing/).
