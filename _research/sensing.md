To diagnose disease early, it helps to sense it **from within**. STORM Lab develops
capsules and devices that carry advanced sensing payloads to the site of interest
inside the body.

## Micro-ultrasound in the GI tract

In collaboration with partners at the **University of Glasgow**, we have helped
create a pioneering capsule capable of performing **diagnostic micro-ultrasound**
within the gastrointestinal tract, under intelligent magnetic control. Bringing
the ultrasound transducer right up to the tissue enables high-resolution imaging
that surface views alone cannot provide.

## Sensing meets autonomy

Advanced sensing is most powerful when combined with precise positioning. By
uniting on-board sensors with our [magnetic manipulation](/research/capsule-robots/)
and [autonomy](/research/autonomy/) work, we aim for devices that can both reach
the right place and gather the right information — supporting earlier, more
accurate diagnosis.
