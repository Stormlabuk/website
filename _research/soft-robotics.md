Rigid instruments struggle to navigate the soft, winding pathways of the human
body without risking injury. **Soft surgical robots** offer a gentler
alternative, bending and conforming to anatomy rather than forcing their way
through it.

## Soft magnetic continuum manipulators

We design **soft, magnetically-controlled continuum manipulators** — slender,
flexible structures whose shape is steered by external magnetic fields. Without
internal motors or cables along their length, they can be made thin, compliant
and safe, while still being precisely controllable from outside the body.

## Why it matters

Soft robotics lets us reach **deeper and more safely** into the body, opening up
minimally invasive approaches to regions that are currently hard to access. Paired
with our work on [capsule robots](/research/capsule-robots/) and
[autonomy](/research/autonomy/), soft manipulators are a key building block for
the next generation of surgical and endoscopic tools.
