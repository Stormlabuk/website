Colonoscopy is the gold standard for detecting colorectal cancer, yet the
traditional push-style endoscope can be painful, requires significant skill, and
is not available everywhere it is needed. The **Magnetic Flexible Endoscope
(MFE)** sets out to change that.

## How it works

The MFE is a **capsule-shaped device connected to a highly flexible tether**.
Instead of being pushed from behind, it is **pulled** from the front: a permanent
magnet mounted on a robotic arm, positioned above the patient, guides the capsule
gently through the colon. Because the device is soft and led by magnetic force
rather than pushed against the bowel wall, the procedure can be **less painful and
safer**.

## Intelligent, autonomous navigation

Using **computer vision and control**, the robotic system can autonomously
navigate the capsule through the colon — keeping it centred in the lumen and
following the anatomy — while the clinician can always override and take manual
control. This lowers the skill barrier and makes high-quality examination more
widely achievable.

## Toward the clinic

The MFE has progressed toward **first-in-human clinical evaluation**, a milestone
in translating magnetic endoscopy from the laboratory into patient care. Our goal
is **painless, safer, easier and more affordable** investigation of the large
intestine — enabling earlier diagnosis and wider screening.
