overview:
  - "We are disrupting colonoscopy with a new design of robotic endoscope. A soft, capsule-shaped tip is connected to a highly flexible tether and guided through the colon by an external magnet mounted on a robotic arm positioned over the patient."
  - "Because the device is pulled gently from the front rather than pushed from behind, the procedure can be less painful and safer — paving the way to easier and more affordable investigation of the large intestine, earlier diagnosis and wider screening for cancer."
  - "Using computer vision and control, the system can autonomously navigate the capsule through the colon, while the clinician retains the ability to take over at any time."
