Advanced medical technology is only transformative if people can actually access
it. A core motivation of STORM Lab is to make robotic diagnosis and treatment
**affordable** — including in settings with limited healthcare infrastructure.

## Designing for access

By moving intelligence into **software and control** — computer vision, autonomy
and magnetic manipulation — rather than relying solely on expensive mechanical
complexity, we aim to lower the cost and skill barriers of procedures such as
colonoscopy. The result is technology that more clinics, in more places, can
realistically deploy.

## Impact

Affordable, intelligent robotics can enable **wider screening and earlier
diagnosis** of diseases such as cancer, improving outcomes and quality of life for
far more people than today's specialist-only equipment allows. This principle runs
through all of our research, from the [Magnetic Flexible Endoscope](/research/mfe/)
to our work on [autonomy](/research/autonomy/).
