A new paper from the STORM Lab UK presented at Digestive Disease Week 2020, and now appearing in _Gastroenterology_ (May 2020), describes initial usability testing of our novel low-cost gastroscope ([the BellowScope](https://www.stormlabuk.com/research/bellowscope/)) with medical professionals in rural India. The research is part of our collaboration with the [NIHR funded Global Health Research Group – Surgical Technologies](https://ghrgst.nihr.ac.uk/news/arsicon-2019/). The work was identified as a _Poster of Distinction _within the event, and forms the basis of ongoing research into the application of our low-cost endoscope technology in low-resource settings.
