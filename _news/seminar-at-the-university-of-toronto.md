16 February 2018. Pietro Valdastri was invited to give the talk “Lifesaving Capsule Robots” at the University of Toronto, within the Distinguished Seminar Series of the [Department of Mechanical and Industrial Engineering](http://www.mie.utoronto.ca/). During the visit, Pietro had a chance to visit several great research labs, including Jim Drake's Lab at SickKids Hospital (picture below) and Eric Diller's [Microrobotics Laboratory](http://microrobotics.mie.utoronto.ca/).
