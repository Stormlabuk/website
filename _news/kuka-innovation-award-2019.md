The STORM Lab has been selected to participate in the KUKA Innovation Award 2019 as one of five finalists. This award brings focus to "Healthy Living" using robotics, in this case using the LBR iiwa medical grade robot supplied by KUKA. For this award we will be further developing our magnetic capsule endoscope for painless colonoscopy, of which you can read more about (http://stormlabuk.com/capsule_robots/magnetic_colonoscopy/). We will be incorporating a roboception 3d vision sensor to further enhance the safety and performance of our system and look forward to meeting the other finalists and presenting our work to the panel of expert judges in Hannover, Germany later next year!
