---
title: "Magnetic Flexible Endoscope reaches first-in-human evaluation"
date: 2025-01-01
author: "STORM Lab UK"
---

The **Magnetic Flexible Endoscope (MFE)** — a soft, capsule-tipped endoscope
guided through the colon by an external magnet on a robotic arm — has progressed
toward **first-in-human clinical evaluation**, an important milestone in bringing
magnetic endoscopy from the laboratory toward patient care.

The MFE aims to make colonoscopy **painless, safer, easier and more affordable**,
supporting earlier diagnosis and wider screening for colorectal cancer.
