The STORM Lab has commenced its first-in-human study of a magnetically actuated flexible endoscope for colorectal cancer screening, conducted in partnership with clinical collaborators.

Unlike conventional colonoscopy, which pushes the instrument from behind, the magnetic endoscope is pulled gently from its tip by an externally controlled magnetic field — reducing looping, pain and the need for sedation.
