In this collaborative paper with Prof Simaan’s group at Vanderbilt University, we explore for the first time how to implement full-shape closed loop control of a magnetic tentacle robot using fibre Bragg grating sensors. We demonstrate that the proposed controller, running at approximately 300 Hz, is capable of shape tracking with a mean error of 8.5% and maximum error of 35.2%.
