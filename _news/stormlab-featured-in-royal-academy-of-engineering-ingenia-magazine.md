We are thrilled to announce that our work on magnetically guided colonoscopy has been featured in the [March 2023 issue of Ingenia Magazine](https://eur03.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.ingenia.org.uk%2Fingenia%2Fissue-94%2Finnovation-watch&data=05%7C01%7CS.Wilson2%40leeds.ac.uk%7Cccf301da8167452951e708db1fff2fc1%7Cbdeaeda8c81d45ce863e5232a535b7cb%7C1%7C0%7C638138953092763270%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJWIjoiMC4wLjAwMDAiLCJQIjoiV2luMzIiLCJBTiI6Ik1haWwiLCJXVCI6Mn0%3D%7C3000%7C%7C%7C&sdata=dk%2BASebi9VP7AdvC%2Fh3nDT6Iz8znCPkMCn5XxItXo%2BY%3D&reserved=0), published by the Royal Academy of Engineering. The innovative instrument developed by our research team at the STORM Laboratory, University of Leeds, uses intelligent and autonomous magnetic manipulation to make colonoscopies more intuitive and easier to guide. Led by Professor Pietro Valdastri, our team has been perfecting this alternative to the conventional colonoscopy system since 2008. We are proud to have our work recognized by the Royal Academy of Engineering, and we will continue to work towards advancing the field of medical robotics.
