A new closed-loop control result from the lab demonstrates sub-millimetre positioning of a magnetically guided capsule within the gastrointestinal tract, bringing in-situ diagnostic micro-ultrasound under precise external control closer to clinical use.

Developed with collaborators at the University of Glasgow, the work couples real-time localisation with magnetic actuation so the capsule can hold and adjust its position against tissue for high-resolution imaging.
