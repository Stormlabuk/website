Published in IEEE Transactions on Robotics, this work demonstrates how the powerful, precisely controlled magnetic field of an MRI scanner can both steer and power miniature instruments deep within tissue, removing the need for separate external actuation hardware.

Performing guidance and imaging within the same system opens a route to image-guided intervention that is both accurate and radiation-free.
