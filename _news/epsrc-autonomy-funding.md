The lab has been awarded new EPSRC funding to advance autonomy in surgical robotics, supporting research into shared-control and autonomous behaviours that reduce operator burden and widen access to complex procedures.

The programme builds on the lab's expertise in magnetic manipulation, control and computer vision, keeping the clinician in command while the system handles moment-to-moment navigation.
