A leading academic at the University of Leeds has been honoured for his work in developing medical robots.

Pietro Valdastri, Professor of Robotics and Autonomous Systems in the [School of Electronic and Electrical Engineering](https://eps.leeds.ac.uk/electronic-engineering) and Director of the [STORM Lab](https://www.stormlabuk.com/), has been made a Fellow of the [Institute of Electrical and Electronic Engineers](https://www.ieee.org/) (IEEE).

Fellowships are given to a limited shortlist of members who have made significant contributions to engineering, science and technology.

More details can be found on the University of Leeds news article:
