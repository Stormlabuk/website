We’re excited to share that StormLab UK has been featured on **BBC’s** children’s medical programme, _Operation Ouch_!

The episode features our work while exploring how blood carries oxygen around the body. Dr Tosin sends Dr Xand and Dr Chris through giant blood vessel tunnels to discover how this vital process works, bringing healthcare science and innovation to life in a fun and accessible way.
